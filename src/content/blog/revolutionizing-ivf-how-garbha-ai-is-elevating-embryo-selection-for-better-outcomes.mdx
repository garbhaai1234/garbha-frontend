Infertility continues to challenge millions worldwide, affecting not only biological hopes but emotional and financial stability. As IVF (In Vitro Fertilization) becomes the leading solution for many, the selection of the right embryo is critical to treatment success. However, traditional embryo assessments depend heavily on subjective visual evaluations by embryologists, often leading to inconsistencies.

To address this challenge, researchers at Garbha.ai have pioneered a deep learning-based solution that revolutionizes embryo grading. By automating the evaluation process, Garbha.ai enhances precision, boosts IVF success rates, and offers renewed hope to individuals and couples pursuing parenthood.

- **Data-Driven Insights:** Garbha.ai trained its AI model on a massive dataset of over 100,000 microscopic embryo images, representing days 1 to 5 of embryo development.
- **Advanced AI Models:** Initially, their ResNet50-based model achieved 83% training accuracy and 81% testing accuracy at the time of research publication. With continuous advancements, Garbha.ai's model now delivers an outstanding 94% accuracy in embryo quality prediction.
- **Real-Time Predictions:** Using a Streamlit-powered web application, clinicians can upload embryo images and instantly receive quality predictions — optimizing embryo selection in real-time.
- **Structured Approach:** The CRISP-ML(Q) framework ensured rigorous data collection, annotation, model building, deployment, and ongoing monitoring to maintain system reliability and robustness.

## Impact on Fertility Treatments

Garbha.ai's innovation accelerates embryo selection, enhances pregnancy success rates, reduces healthcare costs, and introduces objectivity into IVF labs. By accurately identifying the most viable embryos, the technology reduces the need for multiple IVF cycles, easing both the emotional and financial burden on patients.

## Looking Ahead

With 94% accuracy achieved, Garbha.ai is setting a new standard for embryo assessment. Future developments include expanding clinical validations, enhancing model interpretability, and ensuring responsible AI usage with full transparency and patient consent. Their mission is to make fertility treatments smarter, more accessible, and more successful for all.
