# SEO and AI-search baseline, 12 October 2026

Measured, not estimated. Live site = `origin/main` e98c597 (the two `/care` branches not yet deployed).
Re-run the crawl with `python tools/seo/crawl.py https://garbha.ai`.

## What could not be measured

No Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, analytics or rank tracker is connected to garbha.ai,
so rankings, impressions, clicks, indexed-page counts, conversions and real-user Core Web Vitals
(field data) have **no baseline yet**. Stage 0 sets up Search Console and Bing
(`search-console-setup.md`); their first 28 days become the traffic baseline.

## Crawl (live, all 33 sitemap URLs)

- 33/33 return 200; one H1 each; canonical = own URL; `index, follow`; `lang="en"`; unknown URLs 404.
- robots.txt `Allow: /` + sitemap; sitemap has `lastmod` only on blog posts.
- Redirects: `http://` and `www` → `https://garbha.ai` in one hop, except `http://www…` (two hops).
  Trailing slash → 308 to the slashless URL. Old WordPress paths (`/about-us`, `/feed`…) end in 404;
  none of them were seen in search results.
- No `llms.txt` (404).

## Search landscape (WebSearch, US-located, 23 queries; positions are not Google ranks)

- garbha.ai appeared for none of the 23 queries. Brand queries returned press releases (Tribune
  India, NewsX, The Daily Guardian) about the Aansh IVF partnership, not garbha.ai.
- Name competition: GarbhaGudi IVF (Bangalore clinic chain), GARBH-INi (government research).
- Weak or academic results (realistic): AI sperm selection ICSI, oocyte quality AI, embryo grading
  consistency, time-lapse embryo AI, AI embryo grading India, ART Act / DPDP for clinics.
- Spam-heavy but medical (needs a named medical reviewer): ovulation / fertile-window calculator,
  embryo grading chart, semen analysis WHO 2021 values.
- Held by hospital chains (not realistic now): IVF cost Hyderabad/India, success rate by age,
  best IVF centre, egg-freezing cost, IUI vs IVF.
- AI answer engines could not be queried; by proxy they cite journals, NHS, clinic chains,
  legal publishers and trade news.
